Machine & fabrication shop · Poughkeepsie, New York

Marine machining, billet to powder coat.

Technical Precision machines and fabricates parts for boats: hardware, brackets, plates, fittings and one-off replacements. CNC machines, punching, press brake bending, welding and wide-belt sanding are all in the shop, and powder coating is done in the same building.

Boats come first here, but the shop also makes parts for cars, aircraft, brewery equipment, scientific instruments and just about anything else made of metal.

Sheet 02 · Marine work

Parts for boats, made in one shop.

Salt water, vibration and sun are hard on hardware. When a bracket cracks, a cleat pulls loose or a part simply isn't made anymore, Technical Precision can machine a new one from solid stock, form and weld it from plate, and finish it with powder coat without it leaving the building.

    Sheet 04 · Capabilities

    Machined, punched, bent, welded, finished.

    Every step from raw stock to a coated part happens under one roof, so a job doesn't bounce between vendors.

    A

    CNC machining

    CNC machines cut parts from solid billet: profiles, pockets, bores and faces milled from bar and plate stock.

    B

    Drilling & tapping

    Drilled, counterbored, countersunk and tapped holes, from a single mounting hole to a full grid of threaded holes.

    C

    Punching

    Punching machines put holes, slots and cutouts in sheet metal quickly and repeatably, ready for bending.

    D

    Press brake bending

    Press brake bending turns flat, punched blanks into brackets, channels, angles and enclosures.

    E

    Welding

    Welded brackets, frames and assemblies, with gussets, tabs and fittings joined right in the shop.

    F

    Wide-belt sanding

    A wide-belt sander takes flat parts to a clean, even surface with a straight, uniform grain, and knocks down burrs.

    G

    Powder coating

    A powder coater in the same building finishes parts in a durable coat, so machined and fabricated parts come back finished from one stop.

      Sheet 06 · About

      A Poughkeepsie machine shop.

      Technical Precision Corp. is a machine shop at 22 Commerce St. in Poughkeepsie, New York, in Dutchess County.

      The shop runs CNC machines for billet work, alongside punching machines, press brakes, welding and a wide-belt sander. A powder coater works in the same building, so a part can go from raw stock to a finished coat in one stop.

      Marine work leads. Past work also includes parts for IBM and for brewery equipment, scientific equipment, aircraft and cars.
